# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 24, 2026_

Delphinai Corporation ("Delphinai", "we", "us") operates this website at
[delphinai.ca](https://delphinai.ca). This policy explains what information is
collected when you visit it and how that information is used.

## Information we collect

This website is a static informational site. It has no user accounts, contact
forms, or analytics tracking, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information through it.

## Third-party services

- **Hosting.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical
  information such as your IP address when you visit, for security and service
  operation. See the
  [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Fonts.** Some pages load web fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your
  IP address when the fonts are requested. See the
  [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Cookies and local storage

We do not set tracking cookies. Your browser may store small preferences (such
as light or dark mode) locally on your device. This data is not sent to us.

## Contacting us

If you contact us by email, we use the information you provide only to respond
to you. We do not sell or share it.

## Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial law, you may ask
what personal information we hold about you and request that it be corrected.
To make a request, or for any question about this policy, contact us at
_info **at** delphinai.ca_.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page
shows when it was last revised.
